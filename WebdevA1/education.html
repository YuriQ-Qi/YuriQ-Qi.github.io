Hi! I am excited to share with you about my matters regarding to my education. Here are the details

Primary School

I attended Yumin Primary school from year 2014 to 2019. I am fond of some teachers there and I am really grateful of my Primary 5 Mathematics teacher, Mdm Goh. I wouldn't be so outgoing, outspoken and confident if she hadn't gave me chances to speak up in class, either answering a question or going up to the whiteboard to write my answers. Though she is very strict, but she still made an impact in my life. I am one of the strongest student there for Mother Tongue. Usually the top few for Normal Mother Tongue in the cohort.

CCA

I attended the Library Club after being rejected from Art Club. My job is to arrange the books in the library, to arrange the new school newsletters and articles, or to run up and down the school to each classroom to record the names of the people who has overdue books. I also get to have a sneak peek of the newest school's newsletters before it gets distributed to the rest of the schools as one of the task in the CCA is to help package these newsletters 2 weeks before it gets distributed to the whole school.

About the CCA

Students in the library club not only help with the administration matters in the library, but also assist in roles like packaging newsletters or newspaper articles before the distribution to rest of the school.

Secondary School

I went to Tampines Secondary School from year 2020 to 2023. Life is really up and down when I was studying there. Some of the teachers were really caring and nice like my A-math teacher, Mr John Lim. Whilst there are some not so great teachers who taught my class as well...so it is 2 extremes. I had also won several Edusave awards and an Eagle award during my time there.

CCA

I got into the Art Club after passing the CCA trial for the club. After participating in all the competitions and helping the teachers around in the art room, I got chosen as the vice-president of the Art Club. An Eagle Award was given to me for my contributions after I graduated.

EAGLES Award

EAGLES award is an Edusave Award for Achievement, Good Leadership and Service (EAGLES). EAGLES is awarded to students who have demonstrated leadership qualities, service to community and schools, excellence in non-academic activities, and good conduct.

Edusave Awards categories

Edusave Good Progress Award

Edusave Good Progress Award (GPA) is awarded to students who are within the top 10% of their institution's level and course in terms of improvement in academic performance and have demonstrated good conduct.
