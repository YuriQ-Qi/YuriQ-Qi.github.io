Hi! These are my hobbies that I absolutely adore!

Mai Mai

Maimai is a rhythm game that is only available in arcades. It tests one's skills and accuracy. I find it enjoyable when I play more difficult songs and get better accuracy. Moreover, I get to also exercise and socialise with other players through it!

Collecting trading cards

I have been collecting cards since I was a child as it brings me exhilaration from the surprise that comes with opening every pack. However, it became harder to acquire these cards as other brands quickly surpasses in popularity. Nevertheless, this hobby allows me to collect rare cards that I can show my little cousin.

Gaming

I absolutely love to play games, though I know when to stop playing so much games and when I could do so. It helps me relief stress after a long day as well. I play many different types of games, like Tower defence games, (multiplayer) Role-play games like Genshin Impact, rogue-like games like Holocure and many rhythm games like BanG Dream, Arcaea, Phigros as well as Rhythm Plus.
